Enhanced Trade & Commerce

The Dhaka Bypass Expressway creates a vital economic corridor that enhances trade flow between key industrial zones and major ports. By directly connecting Gazipur's manufacturing hubs with southern ports via Narayanganj, the expressway reduces freight transit times by up to 75%.

This improved connectivity is projected to increase exports by 0.8% of GDP as transportation costs decrease and reliability increases, making Bangladeshi products more competitive in global markets.

Key Benefits for Trade

  • Reduced transport costs (estimated 15-20% reduction)
  • Improved just-in-time delivery capabilities
  • Enhanced access to global markets via Chattogram Port

Regional Development Impact

The expressway is transforming areas along its route, creating new economic hubs and opportunities

Urban Development Zones

Areas adjacent to the expressway are experiencing significant urban development. The Purbachal New Town project, directly accessible from the expressway, is becoming a major satellite city with commercial and residential developments.

Key Growth Areas:

  • Purbachal Economic Zone - 46% land value increase
  • Gazipur Industrial Corridor - 38% business growth
  • Narayanganj Logistics Hub - 52% capacity expansion

Long-Term Economic Benefits

The economic impact extends beyond immediate construction benefits. The expressway creates a foundation for sustainable economic growth throughout eastern Bangladesh over the next 25+ years.

Projected Long-Term Effects:

  • Decentralization of economic activity from Dhaka core
  • Creation of specialized industrial clusters along the route
  • Enhanced property values and local tax revenues
  • Integration with other planned infrastructure projects
