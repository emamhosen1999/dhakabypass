China-Bangladesh Cooperation

The Dhaka Bypass Expressway project represents a landmark achievement in China-Bangladesh cooperation, bringing together Chinese investment, technical expertise, and experience with local Bangladeshi knowledge and resources.

Led by Sichuan Road & Bridge Group (SRBG) as the majority partner with a 60% stake in the project company, Chinese contribution encompasses financing, engineering expertise, advanced construction methods, and knowledge transfer that is helping to transform Bangladesh's infrastructure landscape.

This partnership demonstrates how the Belt and Road Initiative is creating practical benefits through infrastructure development. By introducing innovative technologies like semi-rigid pavement and reinforced retaining walls for the first time in Bangladesh, Chinese partners are not just building a road but establishing new standards for infrastructure throughout the country.

Case Study: Semi-Rigid Pavement Technology

Introducing revolutionary road construction techniques to Bangladesh

Technology Transfer

One of the most significant Chinese contributions to the Dhaka Bypass Expressway project has been the introduction of semi-rigid pavement technology to Bangladesh. This innovative construction method combines the flexibility of asphalt with the strength of concrete to create a more durable and longer-lasting road surface.

SRBG has not only implemented this technology but has provided comprehensive training to Bangladeshi engineers, both through specialized programs in China and hands-on training at the project site. This knowledge transfer will have lasting benefits for infrastructure development throughout Bangladesh.

Benefits for Bangladesh

Increased Durability

Extends road life by up to 30% compared to conventional methods

Local Materials

Utilizes Bangladesh's abundant fine sand resources effectively

Reduced Maintenance

Lower lifecycle costs through less frequent repairs

Environmental Benefits

Reduces use of traditional brick materials, promoting sustainability

The semi-rigid pavement technology introduced by SRBG is perfectly suited to Bangladesh's climate, geological conditions, and available materials. This innovation will have lasting impact on our infrastructure development.

— Bangladeshi Highway Engineering Expert

Belt and Road Initiative

Part of the Belt and Road Initiative

The Dhaka Bypass Expressway stands as a flagship project of the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) in Bangladesh, representing practical cooperation that creates tangible benefits for the local population and economy.

As highlighted at the Third Belt and Road Forum, this project exemplifies how Chinese expertise and investment can work within local frameworks to deliver infrastructure that meets international standards while respecting local needs and conditions.

By improving connectivity not only within Bangladesh but also to the broader regional transportation network, the expressway contributes to the core BRI goal of enhancing interconnectivity across Asia. The project demonstrates how the initiative creates practical partnerships that benefit both China and participating countries.

BRI Benefits

  • Enhances regional connectivity across South Asia
  • Creates a model for future infrastructure cooperation
  • Facilitates technology and knowledge transfer
  • Strengthens economic and diplomatic relations

Knowledge Transfer

Building capacity and expertise within Bangladesh's infrastructure sector

Training Programs

SRBG has organized comprehensive training programs for Bangladeshi engineers, both in China and on-site. These programs have covered advanced road construction techniques, quality control methods, and project management practices.

Through this knowledge transfer, local engineers and workers gain valuable skills that will benefit future infrastructure projects throughout Bangladesh, creating a lasting legacy beyond the expressway itself.

The project has introduced multiple advanced technologies to Bangladesh's infrastructure sector for the first time, including semi-rigid pavement construction, reinforced retaining walls, and modern traffic management systems.

This technology adoption helps Bangladesh modernize its infrastructure capabilities, setting new standards for future projects and enhancing the country's overall engineering capacity.
