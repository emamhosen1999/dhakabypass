writes alt text for a picture they have not seen. That mistake is why the old
site describes a Belt and Road infographic as bridge construction.

people shown in the CSR photography. Recorded here as the basis; images in the

## What this means for the home page

There is enough genuine photography to illustrate the home page properly. No
stock is needed and none is used.

The gap is **resolution**, not subject matter. The largest usable photograph is
1024 px wide and the hero aerial is 686 px. They will look soft on a desktop
display. Originals remain the highest-value thing DBEDC can supply, and the
media library flags every one of these as awaiting a replacement.
