## Corroborated historical facts

Checked on 6 September 2026:

- [PPPA project profile](https://www.pppo.gov.bd/projects-dhaka-bypass.php): RHD
  implements the project; the contract was signed on 6 December 2018 with the
  consortium of Sichuan Road and Bridge (Group) Corporation Ltd, Shamim Enterprise
  (Pvt) Ltd and UDC Construction Ltd. The profile is dated 20 February 2024. Its
  old programme and internally inconsistent approval dates are not carried over.
- [BIFFL annual report 2022, page 88](https://www.biffl.org.bd/uploads/images/reports/BIFFL%20Annual%20Report%202022%20Final%20Update.pdf): first loan disbursement
  occurred in April 2022. This is not described as the date of financial close.
- The older website's financial-close assertion is not established merely by
  the April 2021 loan agreement signatures. No such substitution was made.

The content updates retain these source links in English, Bangla and Chinese.
The existing client decision to ship translations before native review remains.

## Explicit historical-publication decision

The client subsequently said to use old information with an old-data notice
where possible, and ask for latest information after finishing. This supersedes
the earlier restriction to confirmed figures for these clearly labelled archive
sections. Every restored block carries a translated provenance notice stating
that details may have changed and are not confirmed as current. This does not
turn old claims into verified current facts. Empty procurement, land disclosure
and tariff-document areas remain explicit because the archive contains no files.

working live traffic credentials, a current board roster or high-resolution
original photography. Missing content stays identifiable. A fully tested software
release is not proof that these external facts or the overall performance target
are complete.
