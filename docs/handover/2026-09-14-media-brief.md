# Media brief: the photographs, video and 360° captures the site needs

*Prepared 14 September 2026. The site launches with 32 small copies of photographs from the old website (all under 1,100 px wide, most under 450 px) and four public broadcast clips embedded from YouTube. This brief lists what to shoot or supply, where each piece goes, and how it will be used, so a photographer or the communications team can work from it directly. Upload through /admin/media; replacing a picture there updates every page that uses it.*

## General requirements

- **Photographs:** original camera files, JPEG or WebP, at least **2,400 px on the long side** (a hero image is shown full-width on a 1,920 px screen; anything under 1,600 px reads soft). Landscape unless stated. No date stamps, watermarks or logos burned in. Early morning or late afternoon light; avoid harsh midday haze.
- **People:** the public pages show the road, not people. Where staff or road users appear, they must be incidental (not the subject), and anyone recognisable needs written consent. No children.
- **Number plates and faces** on anything that will be shown as a camera sample or a still must be blurred or out of frame.
- **Each photo needs one line of description** (what is in the frame, where, when) so the alt text and captions can be written in three languages.
- **Video:** 1080p minimum (4K preferred for drone), 25 or 30 fps, landscape, delivered as MP4 (H.264) with the original files kept. Each clip needs the date, location and a one-line description. Clips are hosted on DBEDC's YouTube channel and embedded; a poster frame is taken from each.
- **360° captures:** full equirectangular images (2:1, e.g. 8,192 × 4,096 px) from a 360° camera on a tripod, nadir patched, horizon level. Each needs the location and chainage.
