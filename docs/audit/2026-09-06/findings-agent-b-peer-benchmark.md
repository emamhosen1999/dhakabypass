## 1. Verdict — 5 brutal bullets

1. **There is no corporate entity on this website.** DBEDC is a real company — 70% Sichuan Road & Bridge Group, 30% Shamim Enterprise + UDC Construction, a 25-year DBFOM concession signed 6 Dec 2018 with RHD, USD 358.83m project cost, partly financed by BIFFL/ADB ([ADB](https://www.adb.org/news/government-bangladesh-signs-ppp-contract-dhaka-bypass), [BIFFL](https://www.biffl.org.bd/projects/dbed)). None of that — no board, no management, no shareholding, no concession terms, no annual report, no audited accounts — appears anywhere. Every serious peer leads with it: [Mundys](https://www.mundys.com/en/) has Governance + Investors + Traffic Performance + Debt & Rating as top-level nav; [Zhejiang Expressway](https://www.zjec.com.cn/en/) publishes articles of association, governance guidelines, traffic statistics and monthly toll data; [Ashoka Buildcon](https://www.ashokabuildcon.com/) publishes board, ISO certs, QHSE policies, ESG and full IR.
2. **Zero transparency/statutory-disclosure layer, in a country where it's arguably mandatory.** No RTI/তথ্য অধিকার page, no designated Information Officer, no Citizen Charter (সেবা প্রদান প্রতিশ্রুতি), no Grievance Redress (GRS) with a tracking number, no tender/procurement notices, no National Integrity Strategy corner. [RHD's own portal](https://rhd.portal.gov.bd/) — DBEDC's contracting authority — has all six as standing homepage modules, as does [BBA](https://bba.gov.bd/) for Padma/Jamuna/DEE tolls. A PPP concessionaire visibly less transparent than its own regulator is an audit finding waiting to happen.
3. **Toll tables are published but there is no toll *product*.** No O–D fare calculator, no ETC/RFID account signup, no payment-methods page, no toll-violation dispute/appeal path, no receipt/invoice lookup. [PLUS Malaysia](https://plus.com.my/) puts an entry/exit plaza fare calculator on the homepage; [407 ETR](https://www.407etr.com/en/rates) has Calculate Your Trip, Rates & Fees, Pay Your Bill, One Simple Bill, Financial Assistance, Anonymous Accounts and **Dispute Resolution** as named nav items; [Linkt/Transurban](https://www.linkt.com.au/) has Open an account, Buy a pass, Nominate another driver, toll calculator. A tolled expressway site without a fare calculator is the single most conspicuous omission.
4. **No incident/emergency service surface.** No published patrol/ambulance/tow/breakdown numbers, no single memorable helpline, no CCTV, no roadworks calendar, no rest-area/fuel/toilet/EV directory. PLUS runs a 1-800-88-0000 emergency line plus the PUTRI chatbot and live cameras; NHAI runs the [1033 24×7 helpline](https://ihmcl.co.in/24x7-national-highways-helpline-1033/) with centrally logged complaints routed to the concessionaire and status callbacks; [NEXCO East](https://www.e-nexco.co.jp/en/) publishes toll+route search, real-time traffic, construction schedules and service-area info. dhakabypass has a "status" page and advisories data — that is a fraction of it.
5. **No downloads centre, no media kit, no ESIA/land-acquisition/resettlement disclosure, no careers, no ESG.** There is a gallery but no press releases as a distinct channel, no brochures/PDF maps/forms, no logo assets, no video/drone footage, no sustainability or road-safety programme, no jobs. Even BBA publishes feasibility reports, resettlement lists and affected-persons documentation; [Brisa](https://grupobrisa.pt/), [Abertis](https://www.abertis.com/) and [407 ETR](https://www.407etr.com/en/rates) all carry ESG/Community as top-level nav.

## 5. Bangladesh-specific obligations

**Caveat:** I could not retrieve the operative statutory text verbatim within this session (several government PDFs and `dhakabypass.com` itself returned 403 to automated fetch). Treat the items below as strongly-evidenced expectations requiring legal confirmation before you publish compliance claims.

1. **RTI Act 2009 — scope likely covers DBEDC.** The Act's definition of "authority" (§2) extends beyond government bodies to **private organisations run on government or foreign funding**, and to entities **performing public functions under government contract** ([ARTICLE 19 country report](https://www.article19.org/resources/country-report-the-right-to-information-in-bangladesh/); [Right2Info](https://www.right2info.org/recent/bangladesh-rti-act-2009-present-status-and-scope); [MRDI, RTI Act English text](https://mrdibd.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/RTI-Act-English-1.pdf)). DBEDC operates a state asset under a 25-year RHD concession, financed in part by BIFFL with an ADB credit line ([BIFFL](https://www.biffl.org.bd/projects/dbed)). That is squarely within the risk zone. **Action:** publish an RTI page with a designated Information Officer + Appeal Authority (names, designations, phone, email), the prescribed application and appeal forms, statutory response timelines, and a **স্বপ্রণোদিতভাবে প্রকাশযোগ্য তথ্য** (proactive-disclosure) index — exactly the structure RHD uses at [rhd.portal.gov.bd](https://rhd.portal.gov.bd/) and BBA at [bba.gov.bd](https://bba.gov.bd/).
2. **Proactive disclosure duty (§§5–6).** The Act imposes a duty to publish a defined list of information and states that "no authority shall conceal any information or limit its easy access" (§6(2)); public authorities publish via website or other means ([ARTICLE 19](https://www.article19.org/resources/country-report-the-right-to-information-in-bangladesh/)). A concessionaire that publishes tolls but not its concession, governance, complaint process or performance data does not meet the spirit of this.
3. **Citizen Charter (সেবা প্রদান প্রতিশ্রুতি) and GRS.** Both are standard Bangladesh public-service instruments and both appear as first-class modules on RHD's portal, alongside the **National Integrity Strategy (শুদ্ধাচার কৌশল)** and **GPMS**. As RHD's concessionaire delivering a public road service, DBEDC should mirror the Citizen Charter + online GRS with named ONIC and Appeal officers and published monitoring reports.
4. **Toll rates are set by government gazette, not by the operator.** Bangladeshi expressway/bridge tolls are fixed by gazette notification (e.g. [Padma Bridge toll rates fixed by gazette, New Age](https://www.newagebd.net/article/170848/padma-bridge-toll-rates-fixed); [Dhaka Elevated Expressway rates announced by government, Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/322993/govt-announces-toll-rates-for-dhaka-elevated)). **Your toll pages should cite the specific SRO/gazette number and date, link the PDF, and state the revision mechanism** — this is both a legal-accuracy and a trust issue, and no peer-country operator omits it.
5. **PPP Act 2015 / PPP Authority.** The PPPA promulgates and gazettes PPP policies and **monitors compliance and non-compliance of PPP project requirements** ([King & Wood Mallesons Bangladesh guide](https://www.kingandwood.com/global/en/insights/latest-thinking/public-private-partnerships-in-asia-bangladesh-guide-2025.html); [PPP Act 2015, FAOLEX](https://www.fao.org/faolex/results/details/en/c/LEX-FAOC179711/)), and runs a public **PPP Project Dataroom** at [pppa.gov.bd](https://pppa.gov.bd/). Align your project page with what PPPA already discloses, and link to it — the government publishing more about your project than you do is the wrong asymmetry.
6. **Language.** Bangla is the operative language of Bangladeshi public administration and every peer government portal (RHD, BBA, PPPA) is Bangla-primary. Your EN/BN toggle is a genuine strength — extend it to **all** new corporate and disclosure pages, and make legal/statutory notices Bangla-authoritative.
7. **Web accessibility.** The ICT Division's **"Digital Service and Web Designing Guideline for Inclusive Accessibility 2022"** aligns Bangladesh government/public-service web design with **WCAG 2.1**, developed with a2i's Disability Innovation Lab and explicitly aimed at public *and private* sector developers and programme managers ([APNIC Foundation technical report](https://apnic.foundation/projects/inclusive-and-efficient-access-to-internet-services-and-information-for-persons-with-disabilities-in-bangladesh/technicalreport/); [National Web Accessibility Guideline PDF](https://file-khulna.portal.gov.bd/files/dphe.shyamnagar.satkhira.gov.bd/news/43d8e062_7c51_4958_b387_365bdfaca98a/ca024f7a037cafaf88f614a269ccd7b9.pdf)). **Action:** WCAG 2.1 AA audit of the map, toll tables and traffic status (interactive map + data tables are the usual failure points), then publish an accessibility statement.
8. **Lender/DFI safeguards.** ADB involvement in the financing chain implies environmental and social safeguard documentation (IEE/EIA, resettlement plan, grievance mechanism) exists. Publishing it — as BBA does for its bridges — is the norm for ADB-linked projects and is expected under the [World Bank PPP disclosure framework](https://ppp.worldbank.org/library/framework-disclosure-ppp-projects).
