# Public UI and front-end quality audit: Dhaka Bypass Expressway

**Date:** 14 September 2026
**Scope:** the public site at https://dhakabypass.com in `/en`, `/bn` and `/zh` (all 189 sitemap URLs), plus the admin CMS's visual consistency and accessibility, read from the code only.
**Method:**
- Playwright (Chromium) against production, read-only. No form was submitted.
- axe-core 4.10.2, injected from cdnjs.
- Performance API read through `evaluate`.
- Source read at `C:\laragon\www\dhakabypass` (branch `chore/2026-09-06-cutover-and-audit`).

**Evidence tags:** **[verified]** means seen in the browser on production. **[code]** means inferred from the source.
**Screenshots:** in the session scratchpad, `…/scratchpad/ui-audit/` (not committed). File names are given in each finding.
**Not repeated:** the behavioural admin findings in `2026-09-14-admin-cms-journey-audit.md`. Section 3.16 covers only the admin's visuals and accessibility. Where a finding overlaps that audit's A1–A5, it says so.

## 1. Executive summary

The site's foundations are better than most public-sector sites:
- **Tokens everywhere.** No public JSX hard-codes a hex colour, and every block draws on `app/design-tokens.css`.
- **Measured contrast.** Contrast in both themes has been measured and written down.
- **Nearly clean axe results.** Across 22 axe runs there was only one serious rule (tables that scroll sideways on phones can't be reached by keyboard) and three moderate best-practice rules.
- **Sound components.** The FAQ uses native `<details>`, the tabs follow the ARIA pattern, and the video player is a privacy-preserving facade. The toll calculator works without JavaScript.
- **Clean basics.** There are no third-party requests and no horizontal overflow at any tested width. 404 pages exist in all three languages.

Five problems outweigh those strengths:

1. **Garbled text in production (blocker).** 36 of 189 sitemap URLs show double-encoded UTF-8: "–", "—", "°", Bangla and Chinese render as `â€“`, `Â°` or `à¦Ÿà§…`.
   - **Bangla and Chinese home pages:** the hero headline, intro and main button can't be read.
   - **Bangla and Chinese service pages:** the whole camera, alert, toll-tag, fleet, frequent-traveller, video, 360° tour and recognition pages are affected.
   - **English pages:** office hours on the contact page, the 360° tour H1 and title, and road names on the map.
2. **Phones cannot see the toll (blocker).** At 320–414 px the *Toll rates currently in force* table shows the vehicle class and part of the section. The fare column is off-screen, with no visual hint and no keyboard access to the scrolling area. That column is the one number a driver came for.
3. **Main menu partly hidden on phones.** Below 768 px, About, News and Contact sit off-screen in a strip with a hidden scrollbar and no edge fade. There is no search in the header.
4. **Bangla is set with Latin typographic settings.** It gets the condensed Latin display face's 1.06 line-height, 0.12–0.15em letter-spacing and 11–12 px label sizes. Latin and Bangla digits are mixed on the same line.
5. **Trust and media gaps a road operator can't afford.**
   - **No phone numbers:** no emergency or contact number appears anywhere on the site.
   - **Misleading images:** the "camera" stills and 360° tour are photos of people and a ceremony, captioned with bridge and plaza names. They are labelled *Sample*, but they are not traffic images.
   - **Empty media and data:** video tiles are blank navy boxes, and every live-traffic widget reads *Not measured*.

**Overall public UI score: 57 / 100** (weighted, see §2).
**Admin UI score: 52 / 100** (reported separately, see §2 and §3.16).
**Finding counts:** 3 blockers, 19 majors (17 public + 2 admin) and 45 minors (41 public + 4 admin).

3. Add a preflight check that fails when stored text matches `/[\u00C2-\u00F0][\u0080-\u00BF]/`. Add a Playwright check for the same pattern in `document.body.innerText` on every sitemap URL.
